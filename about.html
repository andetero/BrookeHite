About Me

My name is Brooke Hite- I am an extremely organized, detail oriented, business focused communicator.

I grew up on a farm in Minooka, IL where I learned a strong work ethic, business focused mindset, and management skills that continue to guide me in my academic and professional career.

In 2024, I earned my associates degree from Joliet Junior College (Joliet, IL). I graduated in the top 10% of my class, was a proud member of the Phi Theta Kappa honors society, and earned a certificate in management and supervision.

I continued my education and expertise in business at Aurora University (Aurora, IL) earning a double bachelors degree in business administration and communication with an emphasis on public relations.

I have a strong interest in writing of all kinds; specifically, I am interested in research, strategic communication, and public relations. I enjoy creating targeted, meaningful content that connects with audiences.

Outside of my professional and academic life, I enjoy outdoor activities, hiking, and staying active. These values reflect my energetic approach to work and life.

I am always looking for new opportunities to learn, contribute, and expand my skills. Whether it’s through creative campaigns, strategic communication, or research-driven insights, I’m committed to delivering work that is thoughtful, effective, and impactful.
